1. Service

Pulsewatch provides automated monitoring of endpoints you configure, and delivers alerts based on policies you define. We aim for high availability of the checking infrastructure itself but make no guarantee that any specific check interval will be hit exactly, particularly during incidents affecting our own infrastructure.

2. Acceptable use

You may only configure checks against endpoints you own or are authorized to monitor. Using the service to probe third-party infrastructure without authorization is prohibited and may result in account suspension.

3. Billing

Paid plans are billed monthly or annually in advance. Usage beyond plan limits may incur additional charges, communicated before being applied.

4. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, Pulsewatch is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from missed or delayed alerts. Use a redundant monitoring strategy for systems where downtime carries severe consequences.
