Privacy Policy
Last updated June 1, 2026.

This policy describes what data Pulsewatch GmbH ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and monitoring service, and how it is used.

Data we collect

Account data (name, email, billing details), the URLs and ports you configure for monitoring, and check results (latency, status codes, timestamps) for those endpoints. We do not collect data from the systems you monitor beyond what's needed to perform the check you configured.

How we use it

To operate the monitoring service, send alerts you've configured, and process payment. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use check data for advertising.

Data retention

Check history is retained according to your plan (7, 90, or unlimited days). Account data is retained until you close your account, after which it is deleted within 30 days except where retention is required by law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
